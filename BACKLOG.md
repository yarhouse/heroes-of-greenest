Paladin: Abjure Foes, Heroism, Searing Smite and the other spells a fight can't use yet, Faithful Steed, Aura of Protection for allies (today only the Paladin's own saves), Lay On Hands curing Poisoned and Restoring Touch. Wizard: Memorize Spell, Evoker's Sculpt Spells, Empowered Evocation and Overchannel, Shield, Sleep, Chromatic Orb, Ice Knife and other spells with riders. Area spells against every monster in the room. Sorcerer: Sorcerous Burst, the other Draconic Spells, Dragon Wings. Bard: Bardic Inspiration on ability checks (flee), Font of Inspiration's slot for a use, Cutting Words against damage and for allies, Countercharm, Magical Discoveries as two fixed always-prepared spells (today they're prepared like the others), Words of Creation, Charm Person, Color Spray, Sleep, Heroism and Hideous Laughter. Druid: Wild Shape lasting half your level in hours (today until you leave it, drop or long rest), a mixed Multiattack in a form (Brown Bear), Wild Companion, Wild Resurgence, Natural Recovery's free Circle spell, Nature's Sanctuary, Archdruid, Shillelagh, Barkskin, Call Lightning, Moonbeam on the turn it's cast. Warlock: Hex moving to a new foe and its ability-check Disadvantage, Fiendish Resilience, Hurl Through Hell, Contact Patron, Mystic Arcanum, Armor of Agathys, Charm Person and Suggestion, Dark One's Blessing when another hero makes the kill. Only one slot spell a turn when one was a Bonus Action.
